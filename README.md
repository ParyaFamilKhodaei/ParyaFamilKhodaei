## 🎃 About Me

I'm interested in the intersection of **artificial intelligence, human-computer interaction, and game development**.

I enjoy exploring how reinforcement learning, LLMs and artificial intelligence can improve human-computer interaction, gaming experience and computer agents' behavior.

- 👾 Exploring AI applications in developing smarter models.
- 🕹️ Developing a 3D game.
- 🔎 Learning the mathematics and algorithms behind machine learning, not just using ready-made libraries.
- 💭 Interested in imitation learning, reinforcement learning, and LLMs.
- 🌱 Continuously expanding my knowledge of these fields by doing research in an AI lab, working on my game, and taking online courses.

> *My goal is to bridge the gap between engineering, intelligent algorithms, and new AI challenges.*
